African St Laurent, Warped

One authored marble slab, shown three ways — pristine, subtly wrong, and screaming.

Stone Author draws stone as vector geometry, not pixels: veins, specks, fog and warp are a few thousand simple shapes driven by a small recipe. The recipe — a .json slab — is the master, and the render is a deterministic function of it: the same slab produces the same image, byte for byte, at any resolution. These three heroes came off two slabs (below), rendered up to 168 megapixels.

Daylight and psyker are the same slab. One authored geometry, two lights: at rest it's near-true; under the other spectrum the full distortion it was always carrying is exposed.
