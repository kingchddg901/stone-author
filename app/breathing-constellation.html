Three motions run on separate clocks. Colour fades in for 45 seconds and rests for 15, hue turns once every 45 seconds, and brightness rises and falls every 93. They only line up again after 93 minutes; at 120× that is 46 seconds. Each appearance runs the whole spectrum, and the colour it starts on moves a third of the way round every minute.

Within the breath: the stars hide in the rest, and the 93-second clock sets how bright each appearance gets. On its own clock: brightness ignores the rest, so the stars can show bright and colourless.
